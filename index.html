How It Works — Step by Step

1

Add Your Teachers

Go to Teachers tab → add all teachers in your school by name.

2

Add Classes & Students

Go to Classes tab → add all class levels → click "👥 Students" to add students per class.

3

Assign Subjects & Classes

Go to Assign tab → select teacher, choose their subjects and classes → Save.

4

Accept Recording → Share Teacher PIN

Go to Marks tab → click "Accept Recording" → a PIN is generated → share it with the teacher.

5

Teacher Records & Submits Marks

Teacher uses PIN at Door A → creates account → records marks → submits to you.

6

Verify → Approve → Generate Report Cards

Verify tab → Approve marks → Reports tab → Select class → Generate!
